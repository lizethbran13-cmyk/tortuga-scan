        About this grade

        Packaged food and household products use Tortuga’s score from 0 to 100. Under 45 is a Fail and the arrow sits on Avoid. 45 to 59 is a Pass on Limit. 60 to 100 is a Pass on Eat as you like.

        Places on the food map use a different score, 0 to 10. Avoid is 0–4.4. Limit is 4.5–5.9. Eat / favorable is 6.0–10. Tortuga approved needs 7.0 or higher and enough agreeing reports. Thin evidence stays “Unrated — need reports.” That number is not the package score, and it is not a Yelp, Google, or Seed Oil Scout score.

        A brand is a Tortuga Certified Clean Brand only when every product of that brand in this catalog scores above 60. It is a Tortuga Certified Garbage Brand only when every one of those products scores below 35. Mixed brands get neither badge. Both labels are Tortuga’s own, and they cover only the products in this catalog, not the brand’s full lineup.
